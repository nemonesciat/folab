# Getting started

*FOLab is an editor for Fallout: New Vegas. You change the game's places, land and items, and FOLab saves your changes as a plugin (.esp) the game loads like any other mod.*

## The game's files are safe

FOLab never changes the game's own files. Your changes live in your **mod** (in FOLab's `projects` folder) and are written to a plugin in FOLab's `build` folder. Only **Put in game** copies that plugin into the game's Data folder and switches it on, and **Take it out of the game** (My mod page) removes it again.

## Your first mod in five minutes

1. **Start a mod**: type a name in the box in the middle of the World page and press **Start**.
2. **Open a place**: pick one under **Places** on the left (say *Vault 21*), or press **Ctrl+K** and type a name.
3. **Look around**: hold the **right mouse button** and move the mouse to look; **W A S D** to fly (the way you look), **Q / E** down and up, **Shift** faster.
4. **Change something**: click a thing to select it, drag the arrows to move it (**G**), the rings to turn it (**R**), **Del** to remove it. Search for things to add in **Place** on the right, click one, then click where it goes.
5. **Try it in game**: right-click a spot and choose **Play here**. FOLab saves, puts your mod in the game, starts it, loads your newest save and moves you to that spot.

Every change is one **Undo** (Ctrl+Z). **Save** (Ctrl+S) writes the plugin; it's also saved whenever you play.

## Where things are

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Browse** | Every record in the game by kind: weapons, characters, perks… Change any field, make copies. |
| **Create** | Items in plain words (damage, ammo, value…), inventories and levelled lists, models with a 3D preview. |
| **World** | Places inside and the worldspaces outside, in 3D or on the flat map (**M**). |
| **My mod** | Save, put in game, open another mod or an existing plugin, xNVSE. |
| **Game** | The game's own settings: screen, resolution, borderless, field of view… |

## Getting help

**Help** (top right) has a guide for each part of FOLab, the 3D controls, and **Report a problem**, which copies what's needed to look into it.
