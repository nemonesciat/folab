# Items and records

*The Create page for items in plain words, and Browse for every record in the game.*

## Create

Kinds on the left (weapons, apparel, aid, ammo, misc, books, notes, keys; containers, characters and creatures; levelled lists), the items in the middle (yours first), the item on the right.

- **Change a game item** and it changes everywhere it is in the game. **＋ New item from this** makes one of your own instead.
- **Fields**: name, damage, rounds per clip, ammo, condition, critical damage, DT/DR, value, weight, effects (aid effects can be added and taken off).
- **Model**: the item turning in 3D; **Change…** picks another from every mesh the game has. Apparel shows its ground and worn models.
- **Carries / Entries**: what a container, character or creature carries, and a levelled list's entries (with levels and its flags). **＋ Add**, **✕** takes one out.
- **Give it to me in game** starts the game and puts it in your inventory once your newest save loads.

## Browse

Every record the game has, by kind. Pick one to see every part FOLab reads as fields; underlined values are links to other records. **Save** keeps your changes in your mod, **Make a copy** starts a record of your own, **Where it's used** lists what places and records name it, **Place it** hands it to the World page.

Parts FOLab doesn't read yet are kept exactly as they are.
