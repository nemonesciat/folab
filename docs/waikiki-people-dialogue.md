# Waikiki's people: who they are and what they say

Written 22 Nov 2026 at the user's request ("write dialogue and backstory for the people, maybe some extra flair and
personality, and voice the lines and add them in"). Built on the approved names page and the settled story only
(docs/waikiki-decisions.md, waikiki-story-plan.md). Nothing here names what the Missouri's captain and the court hide
from the Kahuna: people hint that something is off, nobody knows or says what.

How it goes in: each person gets a greeting (two takes, by chance) and two or three topics of their own in
FOLabWaikikiPeople, conditioned on GetIsID. A quest person's own quest lines come first: these topics are extra and
never block or answer a quest step (the beachcomber, the doctor, the gate guard and Kuliʻouʻou's people keep their quest
dialogue exactly as it is). Lines are short so they voice well; "(…)" is a stage note for the voice's mood, not spoken.

Threads that tie people together (the "integration"):
- **The supply road.** Albright needs stock from the coast road; Delgado's prices follow the boats; the toll post
  takes its cut; the pirates take the rest. Everyone blames someone else.
- **Fight night and feasts.** The guards love them; Albright patches the losers; Kowalski makes the clubs; Delgado
  feeds the crowd.
- **The pirates' bargain.** Calloway says Waikiki pays the crews to stay away. The court fighters say pirates "don't
  come here". Both are true, and nobody in Waikiki says who pays.
- **Kuliʻouʻou.** People sell people there. The toll post knows; Duke and Tommy work it; Manny is selling his son.
  Leilani is the only one there who's kind, and she's waiting for "a captain's people".
- **The Huáng.** Respected and a little feared; their gate guard speaks little English; Waikiki trades for their silk
  and guns through Linda.

## Waikiki

### Dr. Margaret Albright (FOLabWaikikiDoctor), fifties
Background: a nurse's daughter from Honolulu's old hospital families. She learned from her mother's books and from
twenty years of stitching up fight-night losers. Dry, tired, decent. Trusts nobody who doesn't wash their hands.
- Greeting A: "Sit down if you're hurt. Stand if you're not, and make it quick."
- Greeting B: "(tired) Another one. Where does it hurt?"
- **"Who are you?"** — "Margaret Albright. The doctor, for want of a better one. My mother kept the old hospital's books;
  I read them until the pages fell out."
- **"How do you get supplies?"** — "Up the coast road, when the toll post and the pirates leave anything on the cart. Ray
  brings me fruit for the sick. It does more good than half my pills."
- **"Busy here?"** — "Every fight night. The Kahuna loves a good fight. I love a quiet one. We never agree on which."

### Ray Delgado (FOLabWaikikiGrocer), forties
Background: born above a shop on Kalākaua, still in it. Talks while he works, knows everyone's appetite and debts.
Cheerful salesman, sharp counter.
- Greeting A: "Fish, fruit, whatever's fresh! Eat it today, it won't be fresh tomorrow."
- Greeting B: "Hey, hungry? You look hungry."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Ray. My dad had this counter, his dad before him. Same counter. Different prices."
- **"Why are prices so high?"** — "Boats don't come in, prices go up. Boats come in, the toll goes up. Either way, I'm the
  one you yell at."
- **"Anything new?"** — "Feast coming. The Kahuna orders enough for half the island. I'm not complaining. I'm counting."

### Tom Kowalski (FOLabWaikikiGunsmith), sixties
Background: came over from the mainland as a young man on a salvage boat and never left. Gruff, proud of his work,
grudging about everyone else's. Carves the court's clubs and keeps a handful of real guns alive.
- Greeting A: "You break it, you bring it here. Don't bring me junk."
- Greeting B: "(gruff) Clubs or guns? Make up your mind."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Kowalski. Came on a salvage boat forty years back. Boat sank, I didn't. Been fixing things
  since."
- **"Clubs?"** — "The court fights the old way. Koa, shark teeth, good balance. Guns run out of bullets. A club never
  does."
- **"What about Linda?"** — "(scoffs) Moreno sells armour. Fine armour. Then she tells people they don't need my guns.
  We're friends. Don't tell her."

### Linda Moreno (FOLabWaikikiTrader), forties
Background: a trader's widow who made the business bigger than he ever did. Deals with the Huáng for silk and steel and
with Waikiki for everything else. Smooth, friendly, always measuring.
- Greeting A: "Armour, coats, anything worth carrying. Take a look."
- Greeting B: "You've got the look of someone who's about to need armour."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Linda Moreno. My husband started this stall. I made it a business. He'd be annoyed."
- **"Where does your stock come from?"** — "Koa armour from the court's carvers. Silk and good steel from the Huáng, if
  you're polite and patient. They're both."
- **"What about Kowalski?"** — "Tom thinks I steal his customers. I sell armour. He sells things that go through it.
  We need each other."

### The Kahuna's Guards (at the Royal Hawaiian)
Shared feel: proud, loyal, they believe in the Kahuna and the old ways and enjoy the show. Never suspicious of the court.

**Marcus Bell (FOLabKahunaGuard01)** — serious, by the book.
- Greeting: "The court's floors are closed unless the Kahuna's sent for you."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Marcus Bell. Kahuna's Guard. I stand where he tells me and nobody gets past."
- **"Tell me about the Kahuna."** — "He's the reason there's a Waikiki at all. Big man, big voice. When he laughs, the
  whole floor laughs."

**Luis Ortega (FOLabKahunaGuard02)** — friendly, food-minded.
- Greeting: "You missed the last feast. Shame. Be useful and you'll eat at the next one."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Luis. I guard the door and I guard the buffet. Mostly the buffet."
- **"The feasts?"** — "Whole pigs in the ground, poi, fish, Ray's fruit. The Kahuna feeds everyone who's useful. That's
  the deal."

**Kenji Nakamura (FOLabKahunaGuard03)** — quiet, devout.
- Greeting: "Some places here are kapu. Ask before you go where you're not shown."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Nakamura. My grandmother kept the old ways when nobody else did. The Kahuna brought them back."
- **"Kapu?"** — "Sacred. Forbidden. Not a game to him. Not to me either."

**Darnell Hayes (FOLabKahunaGuard04)** — grinning, a little menacing.
- Greeting: "The Kahuna smiles at everybody. Until somebody crosses him."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Darnell. I'm the one who walks you out when the smiling stops."
- **"What happens if you cross him?"** — "(laughs) You don't get invited to the feast. Then you don't get invited
  anywhere."

**Ricky Salas (FOLabKahunaGuard05)** — loud fight-night fan.
- Greeting: "Fight night's loud. You'll hear the Kahuna from the beach."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Ricky Salas. Three fights, three wins. Then they gave me this spear and told me to stand still."
- **"Fight night?"** — "Court fighters, clubs, a ring of sand. The Kahuna calls the winners by the old titles. Best
  night of the week."

**Kevin Tanaka (FOLabKahunaGuard06)** — stiff, formal.
- Greeting: "Stand where I can see you."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Tanaka. Guard of the court. Address the court by its titles and we'll have no problems."
- **"Who runs things?"** — "The Kahuna rules. His court advises. The Missouri's captain has his ear. That's all you
  need to know." (A hint only: he believes it.)

### Court Fighters and patrols in Waikiki

**Andre Wallace (FOLabCourtFighter05)** — "Walking the hotels. Pirates don't come here, and we see it stays so."
- **"Why don't pirates come here?"** — "Because we're here. (beat) And because the court says so. Don't ask me how."

**Brian Oshiro (FOLabCourtFighter06)** — "Keep to the street and you'll be fine."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Brian. Grew up three streets over. Never been past the toll post. Never needed to."

**Danny Kim (FOLabCourtFighter07)** — "Long shift. Short pay. Still beats the coast."
- **"What's wrong with the coast?"** — "Pirates, catchers, Kuliʻouʻou. Out there they sell people. In here they just
  overcharge you."

**Kelly Brennan (FOLabCourtFighter08)** — "Eyes front, hands where I can see them."
- **"Is the court hiring?"** — "Sometimes. Win a fight night and they'll find you. Lose one and the doctor finds you."

**Mike Sullivan (FOLabPatrolCourt01)** — "On my rounds. Walk with me or get out of the way."
- **"Anything to watch for?"** — "Strangers asking about the Missouri. The court doesn't like questions about her. Neither do I."

**Jake Carver (FOLabPatrolCourt02)** — "Edge of town's as far as we go. Past that, you're on your own."
- **"What's past the edge?"** — "Road to the toll post, then Kuliʻouʻou. Don't stop at Kuliʻouʻou."

## Outside Waikiki

### The toll post (court fighters on the coast road)
The toll is asked but the amount waits until Waikiki's money is decided (nothing is taken yet).

**Isaiah Brooks (FOLabCourtFighter01)** — "Toll's owed on this road. The court sets it, I collect it."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Isaiah Brooks. Twelve years on this road. Seen everything come up it. Most of it I sent back."
- **"What's the toll for?"** — "The road, the court, the Kahuna's feasts. Mostly the feasts."

**Kainoa Akana (FOLabCourtFighter02)** — "Pay and pass. No pay, turn around, brah."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Kainoa. From Waimānalo side, before the Huáng came. Now I stand here. Good view, at least."

**Ben Higa (FOLabCourtFighter03)** — "Quiet road today. Let's keep it that way."
- **"Seen anything?"** — "Catchers from Kuliʻouʻou walked a man up here last week. Court said let them pass. So we did."

**Sarah Medeiros (FOLabCourtFighter04)** — "Waikiki's that way. You'll pay to get there."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Sarah. My family fished Maunalua Bay till the pirates took the boats. Now I take tolls. Funny
  how that goes."

### Walt Pruitt, the beachcomber (FOLabBeachcomber), Hanauma Bay
Background: a drifter who washed up years ago himself, which is why he walks the tide line every morning. Rambling,
kind, half-listening to the sea. (His quest "What the Sea Brought In" stays as it is; these come after.)
- Greeting A: "Morning. Or whatever it is. Tide's out."
- Greeting B: "(cheerful) The sea's been generous. Mostly with junk."
- **"Who are you, really?"** — "Walt Pruitt. Came in on a raft a long time ago. Nobody came looking. So I stayed and
  started looking myself."
- **"What do you find?"** — "Planks, bottles, a boot once with the foot still in. And you. You're the best thing the
  sea's brought me in a while."

### Kuliʻouʻou's camp (after their quest scene)
Pidgin where it fits; the quest's own lines are untouched.

**Leilani Kamakau (FOLabKuliWoman)**, older native islander — the only gentle one in the camp.
- Greeting A: "Sit by the fire. Nobody here asks questions."
- Greeting B: "(warm) You came back. Eat something."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Leilani. I been here before the camp, before the catchers. I'll be here after."
- **"The captain's people?"** — "That thing on your wrist. Only a captain's people wear those. Long time I been waiting
  to see one again." (keeps the approved line)
- **"Why stay here?"** — "Somebody gotta feed the ones they bring through. Might as well be me."

**Manny Pacheco (FOLabKuliFather)** — ashamed, defensive.
- Greeting: "Don't look at me like that."
- **"Why sell your son?"** — "(angry, then quiet) Everybody in this camp sells something. I got nothing else. He'll
  eat on that boat. That's more than here."

**Joey Pacheco (FOLabKuliSon)**, a boy — "Dad says it's only for a while."
- **"Are you scared?"** — "No. (beat) A little. The pirate said I'd see Maui."

**Sione Faleolo (FOLabKuliPirate)** — cold, businesslike.
- Greeting: "Business. Walk on."
- **"Whose crew are you?"** — "Captain Calloway's. You want to talk prices, talk to him. You want trouble, talk to me."

**Duke Nakoa (FOLabKuliCatcher)** — "Keep walking, friend. This one's ours."
- **"Who do you work for?"** — "Whoever pays for what we catch. Pirates mostly. Sometimes people you'd be surprised by."

**Tommy Reyes (FOLabKuliCatcher2)** — "Tribute day's coming. Somebody's got to go."
- **"Tribute?"** — "Camp pays in people. Pay, and the boats leave the camp alone. Don't, and they take more."

### Zhao Lei, the Huáng gate guard at Makapuʻu (FOLabMakapuuGuard)
Speaks little English; proud of the gate. (His quest "Through Makapu'u Gate" stays as it is.)
- Greeting: "Gate. Huáng gate. You walk careful."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Zhao Lei. Shouwei. Guard. Huáng land behind me."
- **"The Huáng?"** — "Old family. Old ship. We keep our word. You keep yours."

### The three pirate captains (split into three people)

**Captain Rhett Calloway (Kāhala camp)**, white American — the dealmaker; buys from Kuliʻouʻou.
- Greeting: "This beach is crew ground. State your business or swim."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Rhett Calloway. I run the Kāhala crew. I buy, I sell, I don't ask where it came from."
- **"Waikiki?"** — "Waikiki pays us to stay away. Good money. Don't ask me who signs for it."

**Captain Marisol Vega (Kāhala camp)** — sharp, dangerous, enjoys scaring people.
- Greeting: "You've got the look of cargo. Careful where you stand."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Marisol Vega. Calloway does the talking. I do everything else."
- **"Trade?"** — "Want to trade? Talk. Want to fight? Try."

**Captain Koa Aiona (Hawaiʻi Kai)**, native islander — territorial, proud.
- Greeting: "My boats, my shore."
- **"Who are you?"** — "Koa Aiona. My grandfather fished this bay. Now I own it."
- **"Everything has a price?"** — "Everything here has a price. You too. Today, you're not worth the trouble."
