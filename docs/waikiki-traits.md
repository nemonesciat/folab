# Waikiki's traits (starting perks)

The user, 25 Nov 2026: "most of them are plain undesirable ... some provide mild bonuses, others larger bonuses
with drawbacks, maybe a couple with extreme such modifiers, make them theme appropriate and compelling". The draft
below was approved whole the same evening ("I like those, let's see about adding them in").

Build: PERK records of the mod's own with the trait flag (playable, trait), the vanilla traits overridden as not
playable except Wild Wasteland (kept: players expect it; the user can drop it). The player picks two, as the game.
Plain stat and skill bonuses are ability effects (safe in nv-rs). Effects through perk entry points (prices, damage,
healing) are marked EP: nv-rs has not been seen running an entry point yet (the Market Day console test settles it);
build them anyway, say so in the hand-over. Script ones (S) hang off quests that already run.

## Mild: small bonus, no catch

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Island Raised | +5 Survival, +5 Sneak |
| Strong Swimmer | faster swimming, much longer breath underwater |
| Fixer's Hands | +5 Repair, +5 Lockpick |
| Night Fisher | +1 Perception, +5 Sneak from dusk to dawn (the night lights' DUSK/DAWN) |
| Two Tongues | the Huáng start one step warmer; the money changer Qianshang's rate better (S, dialogue condition) |
| Kala Sense | +10 Barter |

## Bonus with a catch

| Trait | Gain | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Captain's Kin | court and Kahuna's people start Liked | pirates start Hated, hunt you on the coast road (S) |
| Raised on the Boats | pirates start Liked, cheaper trades (EP or dialogue), walk into their camps | court starts Disliked, the Kahuna's guards watch you |
| Dread Name | starts on the dread side of the second axis: intimidation works, collectors pay up | merchants charge 15% more (EP); soft options (pleading, mercy) closed |
| Soft Touch | parley, yield, mercy checks much easier | dread never rises: intimidation always fails, pirates take you for easy prey |
| Storm-Touched | in storms +2 Strength, +2 Endurance, faster AP (S, FOLabStormQuest) | in fair weather -1 Agility, -1 Luck |
| Reef Fighter | +25% damage with the island's clubs, leiomano, blades (EP) | -10 Guns |
| Iron Gut | raw reef fish and sea water do no harm | Stimpaks heal 25% less (EP) |
| Light Traveller | +15 AP, 10% faster | -50 carry weight |

## Extreme

| Trait | Gain | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Sold and Escaped | +2 Perception, +2 Agility; a court bounty per catcher camp cleared | catchers hunt you all game, ambushes on the roads (S) |
| Kahuna's Debt | start with 300 kala (Caps001) and good gear | the tribute collectors come weekly for a share; refuse and the court turns (S) |
| Outcast of Every Shore | +3 SPECIAL points to spend, an extra tag skill | all four sides start Disliked |
| Half-Drowned | breathe underwater for ever, no rads from the sea | -2 Endurance |

Reputations are the four REPUs (court, pirates, Huáng, FOLabRepGuard) and the second axis in "Your Name on the
Water"; starting standings are set once by the new game's script after the trait screen. Not yet seen in game.
