# Tonight's work: the Missouri and the Natatorium (18 Nov 2026)

What `docs/tonight.md` asked for, done overnight on Waikiki. Pictures are in `docs/tonight-pictures/`, taken in FOLab's
3D view of the real mod. Nothing here has been tried in the game yet.

## Decided along the way

- **She keeps her name:** the USS Missouri, "63" on her bows, the surrender plaque where it was.
- **Her heading:** bow north, toward Waikiki and Pearl Harbor, with her starboard side to the shore. On our map the shore
  at the Natatorium runs north to south, so "bow west, port side in" couldn't work. You chose this option.
- **The blast side:** downtown lies ahead of her and to port, so the damage is forward and on her port side, the side
  facing the sea.

## Done

1. **Aground off the Natatorium.** She now lies about 4,400 units out from its sea wall (before, she was about 18,000
   units away in deep water). She sits on the bottom with her decks level, riding 136 units higher than she floated,
   so a band of her red bottom shows. The sea floor is shaped to her: just under her hull, and raised round her into a
   reef shelf that eases back into the slope. Everything that stood on her moved with her: her lights, and her two deck
   doors, still linked to her insides. The old gangway, pier and marker are gone.
2. **Damage pass (model and textures):**
   - Her foremast is bent back and its radars are gone.
   - Her bridge windows are blown in.
   - Her mainmast snapped and fell to starboard, across the roof's edge and out over her side.
   - Her port 5-inch mounts are knocked askew, her Phalanx domes are gone, and her missile launchers are crushed and
     burnt out.
   - Her fantail crane has toppled.
   - Turret Two is trained hard to port, and one of Turret One's barrels droops.
   - Her port side is scorched from the bow to amidships, with blistered paint. The 01 level's port wall carries flash
     shadows of the rails and one of a man.
   - The teak on her forecastle is burnt.
   - A hole is torn in her port bow at the waterline.
   - Seabirds have marked her director and tower.
   - Barnacles and coral cover her bottom.
   - Plants grow in eight spots of soil on her decks.
3. **The way aboard.** From the street you climb steps up through the arch, then follow a walk over the bleachers to the
   pool's south side wall. You walk along it to the sea wall, cross a timber pier on pylons (eight planks long) over the
   shallows, and climb a scaffold stair tower lashed to her hull (two flights with landings) up to the gap in her rails.
   - **Checked:** before any navmesh I walked it on the real collision. Every goal was reached: the tower's top, the
     quarterdeck door, the bow, the bridge door and both wings, and on the Natatorium the steps, the side wall and the
     sea wall corner.
   - **The map marker:** "USS Missouri", now at the arch.
4. **The compound:**
   - **The wall:** sheet-metal junk walls with sandbags and rubble round the Natatorium's land side. There is a gate in
     front, and a sandbagged checkpoint where each of three roads crosses it, so the street now runs inside the compound.
   - **The park:** Kapiʻolani Park in front is cleared of 134 plants and pieces of litter. Its four biggest trees are kept
     and grown, as the lookout banyans.
   - **The screen:** ironwoods and banyans stand between the compound and the city.
5. **Navmesh** is made again for every cell touched, 33 in all, and for the bathhouse (the game's own navmesh for the
   place it copies).
6. **Far view, Pip-Boy map, save, health check and MO2:**
   - **Far view and Pip-Boy map:** the distant ground and buildings are redrawn for the city and the three hotel
     worlds, so from far off she shows where she lies now, and the Pip-Boy map is redrawn.
   - **Health check:** 0 errors. The 255 one-way doors warning is unchanged from before tonight.
   - **MO2:** Waikiki is installed, and all 5,504 files were checked as present and the same. Her decks were walked
     again from the files as installed.
   - **FOLab.exe:** its own copy of the app now has tonight's model code, so it can't rebuild the old, undamaged ship.
     The map-growing panel is still left out of it until it has been tried in the editor.

## Placeholders

- **The bathhouse** is a copy of the game's Primm Sheriff's Station. Its door is on the south pavilion at the walk.
  The guardroom, armoury and cells are still to come.
- **The pool and bleachers** are left open, for the court of judgment later.
- **The lookout banyans** have no platforms yet; they are just kept and grown.

## Needs a new mesh

- **The forward funnel caved in on its starboard side:** FOLab's parts can't dent a funnel, so it stands whole but
  blackened.
- **A bent tripod** made as one piece: it is faked now with straight poles at angles.
- **Torn and buckled plating** round the hole: it is a picture now, and the hull stays smooth.
- **A broken bridge front:** the windows are blown in as a picture, but the steel isn't torn.
- **The kālaimoku's people's additions:** shacks, a signal mast with the kingdom's flags, a searchlight and the lookout
  nest. These are left for a later pass, as the brief allowed.

## Not done, and why

- **The bridges and overpasses:** a trial run would build 60 bridges, but on our squeezed map they would lie at ground
  level, sometimes buried, with no pillars, so nothing would pass under them. A real overpass needs the freeway raised
  on long ramps over each crossing, or the streets under it lowered. That is a choice to make together. Nothing was
  built.
- **The ridge-road settlements:** these are the vassal enclaves, which the brief says not to start tonight.
- **In the hotels' small worlds:** her copies in two of them were taken out, because her new place lies outside those
  worlds. Their far view draws her where she is now.
- **A turret detail:** with Turret Two turned, its guns' breeches show out of the back of the house. The turret has
  always been built that way, but it shows now.
