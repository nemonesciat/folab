# FOLab 0.6.0 test 21

A test build of FOLab, an editor for Fallout: New Vegas. Unzip it anywhere and run `FOLab.exe`. Nothing is put into
your game until you choose to.

**Download:** `FOLab-0.6.0-test21-portable.zip` (14 MB)
SHA-256: `e3aed35c17e1bd7b0a34f10b395ee3fe387b0e10ec80bf9259891f20e7ff1736`

**Only if you want FOLab to voice your lines:** `FOLab-voice-pack-0.6.0-test21.zip` (31 MB)
SHA-256: `64d41ec9080eed6f9bc87a97401b5987f2c107396ac90d14ef6d7bf465940398`

## What's new

### FOLab is a third of the size
The voice maker's packages were two thirds of FOLab's download, and most people never voice a line. They are now
**the voice pack**, a download of its own: FOLab is 14 MB instead of 45.

- Everything works without it except voicing lines.
- To voice lines: download the voice pack and leave it in your Downloads folder, then open a line's **Voice** part
  and press **Install the voice pack**. Or unzip it into FOLab's folder yourself. It works at once, no restart.
- The Voice part tells you if the pack is missing, or is for a different FOLab.
- You still download the Kokoro voice model yourself, as before.

### Also since test 20
- Doors the game's own size in places FOLab builds, and doors you can pick from more angles.

Everything from test 20 (Open, Save and Save As, world water level, faster saves) is in this one too.
